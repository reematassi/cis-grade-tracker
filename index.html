How to use the Assessment Planner

1Find your course

Click Find a course to list every course, or type a code or name (e.g. CIS-4913 or capstone) and pick it. Once chosen, the Faculty box lists only the instructors teaching that course — pick one to show just their sections, or leave it on All faculty.

2Select a section (CRN)

The matching sections appear below. Each card shows the CRN, campus, instructor and class size. Use the Filter box to narrow by CRN, instructor or campus. Click a card to open it.

3Fill in the assessment data

Each section has five blocks — CW1, CW2, CW3, Final Assessment and Final Grade — plus a Comments box. Click a block header to expand it and complete these fields:

FieldWhat to enter
Assessment ToolType to search the list of 18 tools, then pick one.
WeekWeek01 to Week16.
WeightThe assessment’s weighting (%).
% A/A-, B+/B/B-, FGrade distribution (%).
Post-moderation doneYes or No.

4Saving

Your work is saved automatically as you type — the Saved mark appears top-right, and sections with data get a green check in the list. Use Clear to reset the current section.

When the app is connected to your Google Sheet, every change also syncs there automatically. Otherwise use Export to keep a copy.

5Where your data goes

Everything you enter is saved to the program’s central record automatically — you don’t need to download or send anything. Only the Program Chair can export the combined data.

6Collecting data from several people

When the app is connected to your Google Sheet, every section each person saves is written to that one sheet automatically. Open the sheet any time for the master copy of all users’ data, and use File → Download to save it as CSV or Excel. (Setup is in the README.)
